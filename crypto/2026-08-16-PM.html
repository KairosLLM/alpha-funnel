Bitcoin went flat and that is the most bullish signal in the market

BTC shrugged off three straight negatives without budging, Clarity odds collapsed to 10 percent, and the Senate vote on September 15 is the binary that could flip the tape.

BitcoinPolicyMinersStablecoinsEtf-Flows

Friday the question was whether the break under $63,000 was the start of the next leg. Over the weekend the tape answered by doing nothing at all, and in this market that is a signal. Bitcoin is not down on bad news anymore, and that is the read into the week.

Market context. Bitcoin is holding near $63,000 after six failed attempts above $65,000, with spot volume at its lowest since early 2019 and funding still positive. It sits just above the on-chain cost floor near $49,692. Ether is near six-and-a-half-year lows against the S&P. The proxy names are the bright spot: Nubank is up about 10 percent on its first billion-dollar quarter, and the tokenized-Treasury complex is drawing real flows. The week's binary is the Senate's Clarity vote on September 15, with the White House crypto meeting Wednesday.

1. Boring is bullish, and the market stopped caring. Per Milk Road, Bitcoin has gone through three of the worst headlines in its cycle without moving: the Situational-Awareness selloff, Saylor unloading his bags, and the Clarity Act getting punted again. Matt Hougan at Bitwise calls that the most bullish thing in the market right now. Bear markets die in apathy, and the market is apathetic.

The squeeze setup is still loaded, with a cluster of long-liquidations at $63,500 sitting against a thin book, and suppressed volatility is the fuel. The risk is the rollover Benjamin Cowen keeps flagging, that Bitcoin tends to roll over in the second half of August even while stocks make highs. Do not read a stabilization as a bottom. Read it as a market that has stopped selling the news.

2. Clarity is priced for failure, and that is the setup. The institutional odds on the bill collapsed. Per Scott Melker, Galaxy cut its estimate of the Clarity Act passing this year to 10 percent from 75 percent, Matt Hougan calls the bill a zombie that may never pass, and the Senate vote is teed up for September 15. Almost the entire market prices it to fail. That is exactly the kind of lopsided pricing a surprise clears. A pass would be a violent reflexive surprise, enough to push Bitcoin into the high 70s and flip it back into a technical bull market overnight.

The White House hosts crypto and prediction-market executives Wednesday, the event that could move that number. The house read stays the same as all week: Clarity's failure is priced, and the upside of a pass is not.

3. The miners turned into AI power plants, and Anthropic just paid to prove it. Anthropic signed a $9 billion deal with Riot, a former pure Bitcoin miner, and VanEck's Matthew Sigel argues an AI token is worth roughly ten times a Bitcoin token per unit of power. The miners are repricing from Bitcoin leverage to AI infrastructure, and that is why the Riot and CoreWeave-adjacent names have stopped trading like a Bitcoin proxy. The flip side is real: the AI pivot pulls hash rate off the Bitcoin network and turns miners into bigger Bitcoin sellers. Sigel calls it self-correcting once the economics shake out. The trade is to own the ones that already have the AI contracts, not the ones still hoping.

4. The infrastructure is building while the majors bleed. The stablecoin and tokenized-Treasury complex is the live trade. Tokenized US T-bills grew the most on Solana, up $378.2 million over 30 days, with Ethereum adding $272.2 million, and World Liberty is taking USD1 stablecoin issuance and reserve control from BitGo. Nubank printed its first billion-dollar quarter with 34 percent of gross profit from float, a stablecoin-style balance sheet up 10 percent pre-market. This is the plumbing that keeps growing while price goes nowhere, and it is the closest thing this market has to a structural bid.

Single names worth your watchlist.

  • $BTC. At about $63,000 below every major moving average, apathetic to three bad headlines, with the $63,500 short-liquidations cluster as the squeeze trigger. The September 15 Clarity vote is the binary.
  • $MSTR. Saylor is still selling into buybacks while the market stops reacting. The premium is the position and it keeps compressing.
  • $RIOT. Anthropic's $9 billion deal makes it the cleanest proof the miners repriced from Bitcoin to AI. It is no longer a Bitcoin proxy, it is an AI-revenue name.
  • $COIN. A named guest at Wednesday's White House meeting, the clearest regulatory tailwind it has had in months, with Clarity the swing.
  • $NU. First billion-dollar quarter, 34 percent of gross profit from float, up 10 percent. The stablecoin-adjacent fintech that is actually printing.
