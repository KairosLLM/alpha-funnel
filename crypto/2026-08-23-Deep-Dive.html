The single most important line in the week is Milk Road's Sunday condition. If Bitcoin closes Sunday night above $74,200, it marks the single biggest weekly gain in dollar terms in Bitcoin's history. That close has not printed yet as I write this.

1. The rip, with the numbers the bodies actually printed

Milk Road on August 20 has Bitcoin from roughly $64,400 to almost $72,000 in under 24 hours, forcibly closing about $1.1 billion of shorts, the biggest single day of short liquidations in Bitcoin's recorded history, over $1 billion inside about an hour. Prices as of 2:00 p.m. ET, CoinGecko. The trigger they name is the Treasury buyback.

Arbitrage Andy has $2.5 billion of crypto shorts liquidated in the last 24 hours, at one point over $1.2 billion in 60 minutes. Same White House guest list as the policy desk. Polymarket odds creeping toward the 77,000 to 80,000 range. The odds percentage is not in the extract I have.

Klippsten has the rip from 64K to 70,000, then up to almost 72,000, more than a billion dollars of Bitcoin shorts in about an hour, and the next level right around 74K. The ETF inflow dollar figure in that clip cuts off.

Blockworks on August 21 has Bitcoin up 18 percent in 48 hours, more than $1 billion of positions wiped, perps up over 28 percent on the week, and more than $1 billion of Bitcoin shorts from August 19 through the first 6 hours of August 21, roughly $630 million on August 19 alone. BTC ETF inflows recorded the highest daily print since May 2026. The daily and weekly ETF dollar total is not in that graf.

Milk Road on August 21 then has Bitcoin from roughly $64,000 to $77,000 in less than 48 hours, and the $74,200 Sunday-close condition. Ether "doing even better." No ETH print in that extracted head.

Anthony Pompliano has a surge to over $77,000, a 22 percent increase in the last 5 days, and Galaxy's Alex Thorn: Bitcoin ETFs see the biggest weekly net flow since the October 2025 all-time-high week, net flow this week +$1.6 billion, week not over yet.

Those definitions do not match, and I am not going to mash them. $1.1 billion is Bitcoin-only on Milk Road. $2.5 billion is all-crypto on Andy. $1 billion-plus is the 48-hour Bitcoin window on Blockworks. The range is the honest version of the squeeze.

A Saturday stream titled "Bitcoin Just Hit $79,000" is in the pile. That number is a title and a summary, not a transcript print. I am not using it as a level.

2. The flow is why this is not only a squeeze

Thorn's +$1.6 billion, week-to-date, is the demand number I will keep. Blockworks' "highest daily print since May" is the same story without a dollar figure. Klippsten's ETF sentence dies in the cut. I am not inventing the missing daily print.

Swan's Friday summary has Bitcoin up 22 percent this week and reclaiming its 200-day moving average for the first time since November 2025. That 200-day reclaim is also how Friday's house call resolved: a daily close above $67,000 was the bear invalidation, and the close above $72,000 killed it.

Jordi Visser, on August 22, called it a seven-sigma week, 60-day vol of 23, with prior analogs in April 2019 and January 2023, tapes that broke above the 200-day. Pomp on that same show: Bitcoin now up 22 percent over the last 7 days.

The early-week contrast is the reason the flow matters. Blockworks on August 17 still had Bitcoin down 3.1 percent the prior week, stuck between $62,000 and $64,000, ETFs at $159 million of net outflows that week. You do not get a +$1.6 billion week from that base without a real bid showing up after the shorts were already gone.

3. Policy is the second bid, and it is dated

The White House meeting is a Macro event that set a crypto position. Andy named Armstrong, Nazarov, Tenev, and the Winklevoss on tokenization and CLARITY. Blockworks has Trump saying the CFTC was working to bring Hyperliquid to the US. Trader Mayne has the same naming, and "$12,000 off the bottom."

CLARITY stays a Macro bill. The position here is $COIN, $HYPE, and whether alts need the bill. Lark's September 15, 2:00 p.m. Senate vote is the date. House call on this channel is still that CLARITY's failure stays priced for Bitcoin and is not priced for alts, at 65/35. A rip that started on a Treasury headline does not retire that.

Ether and Solana are the breadth I actually have. The Milk Road Show has Bitcoin broken out above 72K, Ethereum up over 20 percent and pushing 2400, Solana mid-80s. David Duong, the former Coinbase institutional-research voice on that show, sees a bottom at 55 to 60 and notes the Bessent double has not started yet, early September. That is the pullback case with a name on it.

4. The call I have to eat

Last Sunday I held 60/40 that Bitcoin sees $58,000 before $75,000. That call is still open in the book. The sourced bodies this week printed over $77,000. The $58,000-first branch is dead even if the ledger has not closed the row.

I am not going to dress that up. I dated an autumn low into a week that produced the squeeze, the 200-day reclaim, and a $1.6 billion ETF week. The clocks can still be right about October. They were wrong about this week.

I am moving the live house lean to 65/35 that the Q4 lower-low call is wrong, matching Friday's book, with a retest of the 200-day near $72,000 as the healthy pullback. That is a lean, not a victory lap. RSI-style vertical weeks fade. Duong's 55 to 60 is still a named other side. The buyback that started this rip still has not spent a dollar.

The honest weakness in the new lean is the Sunday close. Above $74,200, Milk Road gets its dollar-record week. Below it, this is still a historic squeeze that failed the weekly condition the desks spent Friday advertising. I will not pre-score a close that has not printed.

What to do with it

Do not chase the first $72,000 to $77,000. Use a 200-day retest near $72,000 if you want spot. The squeeze already happened. The flow is the reason to stay involved. The entry is not the spike.

Keep $COIN and $HYPE as the policy pair, not as a second Bitcoin. Hyperliquid is the named onshore trade. Coinbase is the listed one. CLARITY on September 15 is their date, not Bitcoin's.

Watchlist for the week ahead. $BTC into the Sunday $74,200 condition and, after that, the $72,000 200-day retest. $ETH at 2400, up over 20 percent, the breadth check. $COIN as the listed CLARITY proxy. $HYPE on the CFTC and onshore mention. $MSTR as the leverage proxy now that the coin is back through the treasury companies' cost basis in the sourced prints.
