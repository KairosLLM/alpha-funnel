Bitcoin held $80,000 through Warsh

Spot Bitcoin ETFs took more than $3 billion over eight days, ether ETFs more than $1.2 billion over twelve, and Glassnode still sees a wall at $81,000 to $86,000.

$BTC went into Warsh toward $80,000 and came out still there. Yesterday this desk had Cowen testing the 50-week and Gillen waiting on an $83,000 weekly close. The speech did not knock the bid out.

Market context. Per aixbt, hike odds rose from 35 percent to 46 percent, the two-year yield gained 6.6 basis points, and the dollar added 0.4 percent. Both coins erased the first dip. The BTC index sat 0.34 percent above $80,000 into Deribit expiry.

Macro owns the hike. This desk owns the fact that $80,000 held it. Per 0xResearch, Bitcoin is up 10.0 percent on the week against gold 1.8 percent and the Nasdaq 100 0.9 percent.

The scoreboard.

  • Ether squeeze thesis from August 20, due Monday: Tom Lee's show has ether running from around $1,900 to nearly $2,600 on the liquidation bounce. The alt side of that squeeze did lead. Hits.
  • Gillen's weekly close above $83,000 on Bitcoin and $2,500 on ether is still open. $80,000 through the speech is not that close.

1. $80,000 held a hawkish speech. Per Cowen, the pre-speech tape was Bitcoin rallies to $80,000. aixbt had the index a fraction above it into expiry. That is the position.

Hike odds went up and the first dip died. I am not re-arguing Warsh. I am telling you the coin that was supposed to flinch did not.

2. ETFs kept buying, and the wall is still $81,000 to $86,000. 0xResearch: Bitcoin ETF flows last week totaled over $3 billion, the first time Bitcoin has had eight consecutive days of net inflows since April 2026. Ether ETFs took over $1.2 billion, the 12th day of consecutive inflows, the first time in nearly one year, not since July 2025. Also last week: $SOL $115 million, $XRP $92 million, $HYPE $50 million.

Per Glassnode, via Scott Melker, $BTC faces a supply wall at $81,000 to $86,000 where long-term-holder breakevens, self-custody cost basis, options hedging, and short-liquidation levels stack up. Thursday Bacon tagged $81,000 and wanted the weekly close on top of it. Gillen still wants a weekly close above $83,000.

The wall is the next tape. The weekly close is not in.

3. Agents are paying onchain again, and $COIN is sitting on the float. Per Milk Road, x402 (Coinbase's machine-to-machine payments standard) saw 8.7 million weekly payments, up from 4.1 million the week before. That roughly doubled. November 2025 peaked at 20.1 million, then the count had fallen more than 95 percent to under 1 million a week by March.

AWS Bedrock AgentCore went live for every AWS customer on August 18. Ramp turned it on for 70,000-plus business customers on August 20.

Coinbase last quarter brought in $292 million from that business, with a record $20 billion of USDC sitting inside Coinbase products, over 30 percent of every USDC in existence. That is the proxy. SOL was about $105 in the letter. Vincent's entry was $82.56.

4. The SEC put dollar caps on a new ICO path. September 15 is still a rumor. Same 0xResearch letter. Three pathways that did not exist before:

  • Subpart B: up to $5 million over four years on a Form NOR, no financial statements
  • Subpart C: Regulation A for crypto, up to $20 million per 12 months, or $75 million with audited financials and a 10 percent income or net-worth cap on non-accredited buyers
  • Subpart D: Form TR off-ramp

That is a framework, and it is the first forward-looking token-issuance map in this window. A Senate Clarity date of September 15 is circulating. Treat it as rumor. Do not harden it.

Tom Lee, on the Milk Road show, called the next five years about Ethereum after the largest short liquidation in crypto history and $500 billion of market cap added, ether from around $1,900 to nearly $2,600. Bitmine is over $14 billion of ether, 65 consecutive weeks of buying, targeting 5 percent of supply. That is color from a Thursday show. It is not a Friday print.
