Bitcoin broke back over $65,000 and forced a big short to cover

The squeeze came with ETF flows turning positive and a named short at $117 million partially liquidated, into the White House meeting tomorrow.

BitcoinPolicyEtf-FlowsStablecoinsMiners

Bitcoin reclaimed $65,000 Tuesday, and for the first time in weeks the move had a crowd on the wrong side of it: a short at $117 million got partially liquidated and the ETF flows turned positive on the same day. The White House meeting lands tomorrow and the Clarity vote math is still on the table. This is the read on a tape that finally caught a bid.

Market context. Bitcoin broke above $65,000 with Per the Crypto list, spot ETFs taking in $284 million on the day, net positive $777.6 million across August 3 to 17, and the largest on-chain BTC short at $117 million partially liquidated as price crossed the level. The move reverses the down tape: Bitcoin is now less volatile than the Nasdaq. Ether is up near $1,900 and Hyperliquid near $593. This is a trading weekday, so the recap is live.

The scoreboard. The week's due catalyst on this book is the White House crypto meeting Wednesday, the call that could move the Clarity pricing Coinbase is a guest at. The house call from the weekend, that Bitcoin needs a daily close above $67,000 to invalidate the bear side, is still open, but the reclaim of $65,000 is the first bullish step and it happened with volume behind it.

1. Bitcoin broke $65,000 and the short got squeezed. The day's signal is that the move finally hurt someone: Gambler 0x8c96's 1,800 Bitcoin short at $117 million was partially liquidated, 360 coins for $23.36 million, with the new liquidation price reset to $65,041. That is the squeeze the range had been setting up, and it is the first time since the 200-week break that the bears are the ones feeling it.

The flows agree. Spot ETFs took in 4,436 Bitcoin on the day, about $284 million, and stayed net positive $777.6 million across August 3 to 17. The house reads the reclaim as the first leg, not the all-clear: the bear side is invalidated on a daily close above $67,000, and the $63,500 to $65,000 zone is now support rather than resistance.

2. The White House meeting tomorrow is the catalyst, and Clarity is the mispricing. Per the Crypto list, The crypto and prediction-market executives meet the president Wednesday, August 19, with Coinbase, Polymarket, Ripple and Gemini alongside the exchanges and the DTCC. John Gillen calls it the market's biggest mispricing: Per Milk Road Show, the entire market assigns less than a 10 percent chance the Clarity Act passes, Galaxy revised its estimate from 75 percent to 10 percent for 2026, and a compromise out of Wednesday's meeting would be a violent repricing.

The setup into it is unchanged: the Senate votes September 15 at 2:00pm, and the market prices it mostly to fail. The house read has not moved: a pass is priced for failure, the upside of a surprise is not, and the meeting is the thing that moves the number this week.

3. The big money rotated into the miners. The 13F filings confirmed a real shift: Per the People to follow list, Druckenmiller closed his Intel and Micron positions and opened four Bitcoin miners, Bitdeer, Riot, Hut 8 and IREN. That is the institutional side of the same rotation Macro laid out, money leaving the crowded semis for the AI-infrastructure miners.

The counterpart is that the miners themselves are still selling the coin. Per the Crypto list, Riot sold 9,665 Bitcoin for $732.46 million in the first half of 2026 at an average price of $75,785, funding the pivot to AI hosting that Druckenmiller is now buying. The narrative is consistent, that the miners are repriced from Bitcoin leverage to AI revenue, and the smart money is following the repricing.

4. The infrastructure keeps compounding while the majors turn. The plumbing story is the live structural trade and it kept building Tuesday. Per the Stocks list, Robinhood Chain launched in July and has already crossed $1 billion in daily volume, with BlackRock's BUIDL and JPMorgan's Onyx building on Ethereum. The house keeps flagging the same shape, that this is the part of the market compounding while the majors trade flat, and it is the closest thing crypto has to a structural bid.
