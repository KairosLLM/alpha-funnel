Bitcoin printed its first daily close above the 200-day since November

A record $3 billion short squeeze pushed BTC to $72,000 while ETH rose 20 percent, ETF flows turned sharply positive, and Clarity odds stayed collapsed.

BitcoinEtherEtf-FlowsAltsPolicyMiners

Yesterday the Treasury handed crypto a bid. Today the bid turned into a breakout. Bitcoin reclaimed $72,000 with the first daily close above the 200-day moving average since November, on a record short squeeze and the largest ETF inflow in months, while Ether rose about 20 percent to reclaim $2,000 decisively. The bear side that ruled the tape for weeks just got its invalidation, and the flows are the confirmation.

Market context. Per the Crypto list, Bitcoin ripped to about $72,000, its highest since June, on the first daily close above the 200-day (a widely watched long-term trend line) since November. Per the Crypto list, more than $3 billion of shorts were liquidated in 24 hours, the biggest squeeze of the cycle, and spot Bitcoin ETFs took in a net $517 million on August 19, the largest single-day total since May 4. Per the People list, Ether rose about 20 percent to $2,306, back above $2,000 with its sharpest day in months, and the proxy equities ripped with it, Strategy up about 13 percent and Coinbase through $172.

The scoreboard. The house call from the weekend, that Bitcoin needs a daily close above $67,000 to invalidate the bear side, is now confirmed: BTC closed above $72,000 and took the 200-day with it. The white-space between the old resistance and the new close is the strongest bullish signal this book has had since the 200-week break, and the market has already repriced the path forward, with Per the Kalshi list a 60 percent chance Bitcoin sees $75,000 within the next month.

1. The squeeze cleared the level that had defined the bear case. Per the Crypto list, Bitcoin reclaimed $72,000 on the first daily close above the 200-day since November, with more than $3 billion of shorts liquidated in 24 hours, the biggest single-day squeeze of the cycle. The largest on-chain shorts got taken out in the move, and the close above the 200-day is the first durable bullish signal since the 200-week break. The house reads this as the invalidation: the level that had kept the bears in control is now the floor.

2. The flows turned and that is the confirmation. Per the Crypto list, spot Bitcoin ETFs took in a net $517 million on August 19, the largest single-day total since May 4, and the combined BTC and ETH flows have been positive three straight days. Per the Crypto list, Ether ETFs added about $178 million in a day and Bitcoin ETFs about $472 million. This is the institutional money following the price, and it is the difference between a squeeze that fades and a reclaim that sticks.

3. Ether is the alt side of the same breakout. Per the People list, Ether rose about 20 percent to $2,306, back above $2,000 with the sharpest day in months, after breaking back above the level on its highest close in months. Per the Crypto list, the ETH-BTC ratio is breaking out, with ETH outperforming BTC nearly two to one on the day, the first time the lagging major has led in a while. The read is that this is a broad repricing, not a one-coin event, and the majors all reclaimed their key moving averages.

4. The regulatory story is doing two opposite things at once. Per the Crypto list, the Clarity Act looks dead right up until a deal gets made, and the odds have collapsed. Per the People list, they cratered from 40 to 14 percent even as Per the Crypto list Coinbase's Armstrong called the September 15 Senate vote the most important next step.

Into it, Per the Blockworks piece the SEC proposed a Regulation Crypto Assets framework with a safe harbor and exemptions up to $75 million per year, and Trump confirmed the CFTC is working to bring Hyperliquid into the US compliantly. The market is repricing the pass odds as a failure while the price action says the crypto bid no longer needs the bill, and that divergence is exactly the setup where a surprise pass reprices the whole book violently.

5. Hyperliquid is the leverage proxy everyone just found. Per the Blockworks piece, Hyperliquid's perps led the day, up 20.9 percent, on the confirmation that the CFTC is working to bring it into the United States in a fully compliant way. Per the Crypto list, the token's daily revenue is back above $4 million for the first time since June, its USDC fee switch is about five days out, and Per the CRYPTO video Stanley Druckenmiller disclosed about a $23 million position in Hyperliquid Strategies. The fee switch is the named catalyst on the book, and the Trump nod turned it from a yield story into a regulatory story.
