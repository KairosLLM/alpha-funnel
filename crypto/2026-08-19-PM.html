Bitcoin caught the Treasury's bid and forced the biggest short squeeze in its history

BTC ripped to $69,500 on $1.1 billion of short liquidations as the buyback news turned the tape, and ETH reclaimed $2,000 into the White House summit.

BitcoinEtherEtf-FlowsPolicyMiners

Bitcoin had been waiting for a reason and the Treasury handed it one. The announcement that long-end buybacks would double flipped the debasement trade back on, and Bitcoin ripped to $69,500, its best day since February, on more than a billion dollars of short liquidations. ETH reclaimed $2,000. This is the read on a tape that finally caught the bid Macro has been arguing for all week.

Market context. Per the Polymarket list, Bitcoin surged above $68,000 and closed toward $69,500, its best day since February 2026, as the Treasury buyback news broke the dollar to a three-month low. Per the Financial Markets list, more than $1.1 billion of crypto shorts were liquidated in a single hour, the largest one-day squeeze of the cycle. ETH reclaimed $2,000 with Per the People list, its highest close since May. The proxy equities ripped with it, Coinbase up 12 percent and Strategy up 13 percent. The White House crypto summit is today at 2:30pm.

The scoreboard. The house call from the weekend, that Bitcoin needs a daily close above $67,000 to invalidate the bear side, is now very much in play: the reclaim of $69,500 intraday clears it, and a close here would be the first bullish signal since the 200-week break. The White House meeting call, the catalyst that could move the Clarity pricing Coinbase is a guest at, resolves today, and the market has already repriced the pass odds hard against it.

1. The Treasury buyback is the bid Bitcoin was waiting for. The mechanism is the same one Macro has been driving all week: the dollar broke to a three-month low on the buyback news, and the debasement trade, gold up 3.5 percent, silver stronger, Bitcoin up 6 to 7 percent, all fired together. Per the Financial Markets list, more than $1.1 billion of shorts were liquidated in an hour, with the largest on-chain short at $117 million among those hit. That is the first time since the 200-week break that the bears are the ones feeling it.

The flows agree. Spot ETF inflows resumed on the move, and the day's signal is that the reclaim happened with volume behind it rather than on a thin bid. The house reads it as the strongest leg up since the range broke, and the $63,500 to $65,000 zone that was resistance is now support.

2. ETH reclaimed $2,000 and the flows turned with it. Per the People list, Ether crossed $2,000 with its highest close since May, and the move carried the whole majors tape. The spot ETH ETFs turned positive on the day, and the reclaim is the alt-side confirmation that the squeeze was broad rather than a one-coin event.

The infrastructure story underneath keeps compounding. Per the People list, BlackRock's IBIT holders redeemed only 0.2 percent of their holdings through the drawdown, a revealed preference that the institutional bid never left, and the fund keeps mirroring the gold-ETF adoption path that took 2004 through a decade of flows. That is the strongest version of the structural thesis, that the institutional buyer is patient even when the price is not.

3. The White House summit today and the SEC rule are the catalyst double. Per the Crypto list, the crypto and prediction-market executives meet the president today at 2:30pm, with Ripple, Coinbase, Kraken and Chainlink among the names in the room. Into it, the SEC proposed Per the People list, a new Regulation Crypto Assets framework, tailored exemptions up to $5 million one-time over four years and $75 million annually, a conditional safe harbor, and preemption of state securities laws, with chair Atkins calling it the most historic step yet to modernize crypto securities regulation.

The Clarity pricing is the mispricing the house has been flagging. Per the Kalshi list, the odds the Clarity Act passes this year collapsed to 23 percent from 90 percent, with the Senate vote September 15, and Per the Polymarket list, Polymarket prices it at 20 percent, down from about 50 percent in July. The market prices it to fail. That is exactly the setup where a surprise pass, or even a compromise out of today's meeting, reprices the entire alt book violently.

4. The miners are the money the rotation left behind and the proxy equities confirm it. Per the People list, Strategy is up 13 percent and Coinbase 12 percent to $164 on the day, the clearest confirmation that the squeeze hit the leverage proxies first. The 13F shift into the miners, Druckenmiller opening Bitdeer, Riot, Hut 8 and IREN, remains the institutional read that the mining complex is repricing from Bitcoin leverage to AI-revenue infrastructure, even as the miners themselves keep selling the coin to fund it.
