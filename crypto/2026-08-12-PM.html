A fourth model puts the low at $56,000, below the leg I have been carrying

Jesse Eckel dates the capitulation to October on a 200-day convergence and rules out $40,000 outright, because this drawdown is already far shallower than 2017 or 2021.

BitcoinAltsStablecoinsEquities

Yesterday three independent frameworks converged on $67,000 as the flip. Today a fourth arrived, ignored that level entirely, and put the bottom below the one I have been carrying.

Market context. Per the Financial Markets morning list, three separate on-chain transfers imply Bitcoin at $63,611, $63,693 and $63,814, so call it $63,700 and unchanged. Bollinger band width is the narrowest since October 2023.

The leverage map is the tradeable part. Per the Crypto morning list, four bears are short a combined 5,375 coins, about $343 million, with liquidations stacked at $64,101, $64,577, $66,007 and $66,030. Two longs hold 1,547 coins with liquidations down at $61,200 and $61,832. The nearest bear stop sits under one percent above spot and the nearest long stop is four percent below, so the squeeze risk is asymmetrically upward from here.

Flows went the other way. Per the same list, US spot ETFs took $865.3 million over five sessions for a 2.2 percent gain, behind the Nasdaq's 3.0, and have now flipped to $144.6 million of net redemptions.

1. Eckel's low is $56,000 and he dates it to October. Per Jesse Eckel, the test is a convergence rather than a level: price and the 200-day moving average meet around October 2026, exactly one year after this cycle's high. Bitcoin has been below that average since October 2025 and was rejected directly on the line the one time it tried to reclaim it.

His path is a drop from 63 to 64,000 down to about 56,000 in an October capitulation, then a turn. The derivation is decay in the post-chop drawdown across cycles: 51 percent in 2017, 20 percent in 2021, about 12 percent now.

This is same-direction confirmation of my 60/40 for $58,000 before $75,000, and it is also a warning about my timing. He agrees the low is lower and he puts it in October, not this week. Note also that his flip line is the 200-day, not the $67,000 that On-Chain Mind, VirtualBacon and Sykodelic all landed on yesterday. Four frameworks, two different lines, and nobody has reconciled them. Per the Crypto list, On-Chain Mind himself today switched to a different tool entirely and called Bitcoin "firmly in the Value Zone" at 60 percent of his DCA channel.

I am holding 60/40 and moving nothing. When four models disagree about the line and agree about the direction, the direction is the information.

2. Cramer sold everything on quantum, and the physics says he is early by a decade. Per Milk Road, Jim Cramer said on August 3 that he is selling every last one of his Bitcoin, because quantum computers crack it within about three years. He had interviewed IBM's Arvind Krishna days earlier, who put the window at three to four.

The research genuinely moved. A March paper from Google Quantum AI with the Ethereum Foundation and Stanford put breaking Bitcoin's cryptography at fewer than 500,000 physical qubits and about nine minutes per key, roughly twenty times less hardware than previously estimated. Caltech and a startup called Oratomic claimed 26,000 qubits over ten days on different hardware.

Then the number that settles it for now. The largest key publicly broken on real quantum hardware this year was 15 bits. Bitcoin uses 256. None of the existing machines are error-corrected at anything near that scale.

The exposure is real and old: wallet formats from 2009 and 2010 wrote public keys straight to the chain, and that plus every wallet that has ever sent is about a third of all Bitcoin ever mined, of which roughly 1.1 million coins are Satoshi's and can never be moved. The defence is already merged, with BIP-360 keeping keys off-chain since February 11 and live on testnet with more than fifty miners.

Cramer selling a 256-bit problem on a 15-bit result is a sentiment event, not a security event. The thing that actually moves this asset inside ninety days is the CLARITY cloture vote in mid-September that Macro laid out.

3. Robinhood Chain is a memecoin chain wearing a real-world-asset costume. Two sources published opposite readings of the same chain on the same day, which makes this easy to adjudicate.

Per 0xSammy, the bull case is that Stonkbrokers fees buy Nvidia and Apple shares onchain and distribute them into token-bound wallets, that a new project called Tickeryard went from a $200,000 mint to $4 to $5 million, and that Robinhood Chain dominates EVM chain fees. He discloses he holds many of the assets he names.

Per 0xResearch, Blockworks measured it. Memecoins are 51 percent of Robinhood Chain spot volume against 5 percent for real-world assets, its stated purpose. Known revenue annualises to $54.8 million, about 14 percent of Robinhood's crypto revenue, and Robinhood's Q2 crypto revenue fell 38 percent year over year to $100 million.

One more number closes it. The Stonkbrokers collection this desk logged on Sunday at $80 million fully diluted is quoted today at $54 million, under a piece titled "Explodes."

4. Two businesses onchain are actually earning money, and neither is a memecoin. Per 0xResearch, Circle posted second-quarter revenue and reserve income of $701 million, up 7 percent, adjusted EBITDA of $143 million, USDC supply of $73.3 billion, up 19 percent, and $14.8 trillion of onchain transfer volume, up 151 percent. The stock rose 16 percent on the week to about $71. Two dated catalysts follow: Arc mainnet on September 16 with BlackRock, Visa and DTCC as founding validators, and federal approval of Circle National Trust.

Aptos is the stranger case. It is the only general-purpose chain with positive token-holder net income, at plus $6.33 per $10,000 of fully diluted value against Ethereum at minus $0.95, Solana minus $1.89 and Sui minus $6.18. It got there by repricing gas ten times, halving staking to a flat 2.6 percent and capping supply. Revenue rose about sixteen times while transactions went from 4.5 million a day to over 12 million at ten times the fee. There is no listed proxy, so it is a token trade or nothing. Carry it for the mechanism: a chain raised prices and kept its users.
