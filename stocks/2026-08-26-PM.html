Nvidia reports after the bell on a $92 billion quarter

Hogue has $92 billion of sales up 96 percent, about 20 times sales and 24 times forward earnings, an average target of $300, and there is no 8-K yet.

This is pre-print. Earnings are after the bell. Per Joseph Hogue, Nvidia reports Wednesday, year to date only up 16 percent, sales up 96 percent for the quarter, $92 billion just for the three months. About 20 times price to sales and 24 times forward earnings, about a 25 percent discount to last year's valuation. It has fallen after every earnings report over the last year, usually by less than about 3 percent. Average price target among 25 analysts is $300, almost 40 percent upside. High target $425. Treat that as his estimate of growth still expected, not a filing.

Market context. Per Stocks with Josh, Nvidia is on a 7-day losing streak, the longest in 4 years, and popped from 207 to almost 215 today. Broadcom CDS roughly 126 basis points. Nvidia about 87. Both record highs. That puts dollars on yesterday's plus-5-basis-point tweet. I am not re-briefing that tweet as the move.

1. Hot Chips is the new token math. Do not mix it with CoreWeave's 10 times. Per Milk Road Stocks, Blackwell token-sale net margins roughly 58 percent, climb to 78 percent with Rubin, 90 percent with later chips. Vera Rubin NVL72: up to 30 times more agentic AI throughput per megawatt, and up to 35 times lower cost per million tokens than GB300 NVL72. OpenAI Jalapeño with Broadcom: 1.5 to 1.9 times using the same electricity as Blackwell, responding up to 3.6 times faster. Google TPU shipments 2.76 million units in 2024 to 8.84 million by 2027.

Per Rick Orford, CoreWeave's first Rubin benchmark produced about 10 times more tokens per second per megawatt than Grace Blackwell NVL72. That is tokens per second per megawatt, not Milk's 30 times agentic / 35 times cost. Different metric. Nvidia introduced 800 VDC on August 11, first racks second half of 2026, up to two megawatts per row in 2027. Japan facility: 27,500 Rubin GPUs and 13,750 Vera CPUs.

2. The financing layer is credit and gigawatts, still pre-print. Per the Stocks list, building 1 gigawatt of data center costs about $50 billion, $35 billion of that into GPUs. Morgan Stanley estimates Nvidia's total credit exposure could eventually reach around $200 billion by 2028. The Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, Brookfield, Goldman, KKR stack is more than $500 billion of outside capital. Orford is explicit: Nvidia has not said it needs to raise $500 billion, and it has not committed to that amount itself. Josh adds a 125 billion potential backstop as phantom leverage.

3. Moderna ripped, Lilly is a trillion, Pelosi bought Intel. Milk Road Stocks has Moderna up like almost 200 percent in one day or one and a half days, 49 percent lower risk of recurrence or death at a $5,000 price tag per patient. Eli Lilly valued at a trillion dollars today. Josh has Pelosi on Intel, 10,000 shares and half a million dollars on leaps, holding into 2027.
