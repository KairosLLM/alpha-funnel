Citadel dumped Leopold's book into the rebound

Milk Road has Situational Awareness at $20.2 billion of US equities on June 30, down from a peak near $45 billion to around $10 billion, then sold to Citadel at 40 to 50 cents on the dollar.

The new plumbing story is not another Nvidia campus. Per Milk Road Stocks, Nebius posted 841 percent revenue growth, Micron guided margins toward 80 percent, and Bloom Energy was raising full-year guidance, and the stocks kept bleeding because Leopold Aschenbrenner's portfolio blew up. That is a newsletter reconstruction of a 13F plus reported block tape. It is not an 8-K.

Market context. No Monday PM lists yet. Earnings are still Wednesday for Nvidia. The new work is who was forced to sell the AI book, what Bloomberg and The Information say about server prices, and three plumbing deals that moved $24 billion in the layer between models and enterprises.

1. The book was concentrated, leveraged, and then dumped. Milk Road has the June 30 13F at $20.2 billion in US equities. SanDisk 28 percent. Micron 27.5 percent. Bloom Energy 9.6 percent. TSMC 6.4 percent plus $24 million in calls. Nebius 6.1 percent. CoreWeave 3.7 percent.

Leverage as high as 4x. Under that leverage the portfolio reportedly lost roughly 67 percent in a single month, from a peak near $45 billion to around $10 billion. Liquidated to Ken Griffin's Citadel in late July at a reported 40 to 50 cents on the dollar. On August 11, Citadel disclosed it had already unloaded more than 80 percent of the distressed position through over 100 block trades.

The house line in that same letter is that their analysts are buying the dip and staying long. Treat the weights as a reconstruction.

2. Najarro's new chip print is 15 to 17 percent on servers, and Poolside at $6 billion. Per Jose Najarro, we are less than 72 hours from earnings. Bloomberg reported Nvidia will raise prices for some of its largest customers by at least 15 percent. He thinks The Information also reported about 17 percent to server makers. Those increases start early next year. He frames it as a memory bill of materials pass-through, not pricing power. There is no real confirmation from Nvidia in his own words. Second print: Nvidia is investing up to $6 billion with a startup called Poolside. Visibility between 2025 and 2027 of over a trillion dollars just on Blackwell and Vera Rubin. This is not yesterday's $105 billion Ohio guarantee.

3. Credo is the named optical print, with no target. Per Edelbridge, DustPhotonics is approximately $850 million to $1.2 billion in cash and stock, with $750 million cash upfront. FY2026 revenue $1.33 billion, up more than 200 percent from $436 million in FY2025. Cash $1.4 billion, zero long-term debt. Q4 non-GAAP gross margins 68.3 percent. ATM $736 million. Free cash flow $407 million against stock-based compensation of $182.6 million. Optical revenue expected to exceed $600 million in FY2027. Shares outstanding about 186.5 million. The price target is behind the fold. I am not inventing one.

4. Broadcom is a $56 billion AI-semi year on the way to above $100 billion. Per Rick Orford, Broadcom expects fiscal 2026 AI semiconductor revenue of about $56 billion, next year around double that, above a hundred billion. Fiscal second quarter about $22.2 billion of revenue, AI semis about $11 billion, up 143 percent year over year. AI networking around 40 percent of AI semi revenue that quarter. Apple multi-year agreement expected to exceed $30 billion, custom ASIC through 2031. Free cash flow around $10 billion in the quarter, roughly 46 percent of quarterly revenue. Top five customers 45 percent of first-half 2026 revenue. Stock range $281 to just under $500, up about 40 percent over the past year.

5. The plumbing layer: Stripe, Marvell, Databricks. Per Jordamøn, last week moved $24 billion into the layer between AI models and the enterprises that deploy them. Stripe acquired OpenRouter for approximately $7.5 billion. Marvell granted Google a warrant to purchase up to 59 million shares at $206.58, worth approximately $12.2 billion if fully exercised, vesting per $500 million of chips through fiscal 2033. Broadcom fell roughly 5 percent on that news. Marvell climbed more than 10 percent. Databricks closed a $5 billion round on August 13 at a $190 billion valuation, up from $134 billion six months earlier and $62 billion in January 2025, with $7 billion of annualized revenue growing 80 percent year over year.

6. Perplexity is a tweet, not a filing. Per the Market list, Nvidia discusses a Perplexity deal at over $30 billion value, per The Information. Treat it as a tweet.
