Nvidia reports Wednesday with earnings tripled and the stock sideways

Milk Road has Nvidia earnings roughly tripled while the stock spent two years going sideways, 75 percent gross margins to defend, and NVLink Fusion as the lock-in for Marvell, Astera, Amazon, and Microsoft.

SemisAi-InfraSoftware

The new pre-print is the margin and the lock-in, not yesterday's 15 percent server hike. Per Milk Road Stocks, Nvidia reports Wednesday, August 26. Earnings have roughly tripled while the stock has spent much of the past two years going sideways. The question is whether it can defend massive 75 percent gross margins. The ASIC and custom-chip bear case is in the same summary. No price target.

Market context. Per 0xResearch, Monday the S&P closed down 0.38 percent and the Nasdaq 100 down 1.18 percent while Bitcoin and gold ran. Per the Stocks list, Broadcom CDS is up 5 basis points to a new all-time high, and Nvidia CDS is at record wide every single day now. That is a tweet, not a vendor print.

1. NVLink Fusion is Najarro's new lock-in, with no numbers. Per Jose Najarro, custom AI chips still need Nvidia's ecosystem. The names are Marvell, Astera Labs, Amazon, and Microsoft. Broadcom may need to adapt. He owns Nvidia. There are no dollars in the summary. This is not yesterday's Poolside $6 billion or the 15 to 17 percent server print.

2. Memory is eight times earnings, and Palantir lost an $875 million FAA contract. Per Rick Orford, SanDisk and Micron both trade at roughly eight times annualized earnings. No dollar prices and no EPS in the summary. SanDisk has quantified future volume coverage and pricing floors on part of the contracted business.

Per Orford on Palantir, Palantir lost an $875 million FAA AI contract to a smaller competitor. The stock is roughly 147 times trailing earnings. His call is moderate buy.

3. Fin Tek's 13F tape is names, not sizes. Per Fin Tek, Ackman rotated out of Alphabet into Meta and Amazon. Burry has puts on Nvidia and Palantir plus shorts via SOXX and QQQ. No share counts and no notionals in the summary. I am not inventing them. Berkshire cash is finally decreasing under Greg Abel. SoftBank: OpenAI, ARM, Intel. Pabrai: Warrior Met Coal, Alpha Metallurgical, Transocean, and new Kaspi.
