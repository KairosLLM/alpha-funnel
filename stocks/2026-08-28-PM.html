Nvidia added $453 billion in a day

The stock was up 8.5 percent, Raymond James went to $515 from $352, and Marvell plus energy buying filled out the rest of the tape.

Ai-InfraSemisEnergyFinancials

Thursday was the print. Friday is the reaction. $NVDA added roughly $453 billion in market cap in a single day, the largest one-day market-cap gain ever recorded in the U.S., and the stock was up 8.5 percent.

Market context. Per the Stocks recap, Nvidia was the most-active options name at 7.4 million contracts, ahead of $TSLA at 1.6 million and $AAPL at 875,000. Software tore. The rest of the session had to digest a hawkish Warsh speech on the same stamp.

The named tape is Nvidia's day, Marvell's guide, IREN's AI-cloud print, and hedge funds overweight energy by the most since June 2024.

2. Marvell printed, and the guide did the work. $MRVL Q2 fiscal 2027 revenue $2.7 billion against $2.71 billion expected, up 37 percent year over year. Adjusted EPS $0.94 versus $0.92, up 40 percent. Data center $2.2 billion, up 46 percent.

Q3 guide $3.15 billion plus or minus 5 percent against $3.03 billion expected. Adjusted EPS $1.10 plus or minus $0.05 against $1.07. Full-year fiscal 2027 revenue roughly 45 percent year over year to about $12 billion. Fiscal 2028 roughly $18 billion, about 50 percent year over year.

BofA initiated coverage on $AMKR at Buy, price target $70. Advanced Products were 82 percent of Q2 fiscal 2026 revenue, with an expected $1.5 billion Nvidia prepayment in 2027. EPS $1.51 in 2025 to $3.41 in 2028, a 31 percent CAGR, and the $70 target sits on 21 times 2028 earnings. Packaging riding the same buildout.

3. Hedge funds are the most overweight energy since June 2024. Same recap: they bought the most global energy stocks in nearly 4 years last week, the 12th weekly purchase over the last 13 weeks. They are now the most overweight energy relative to global stocks since June 2024. That reverses February 2026, when they were the most underweight energy since 2021.

The only stretch with materially higher energy exposure was the 2022 energy bull market. This is the non-AI bid on a day Nvidia ate the headline. $TE is the named energy equity in that recap, on a Giga Arctic Norway story with no hard print.

4. Corporate profits hit a record $4.8 trillion. U.S. corporate profits surged 22.8 percent year over year in Q2 2026 to a record $4.8 trillion, the largest annual increase since Q4 2021. Excluding the pandemic recovery and the post-2008 rebound, that is the strongest year-over-year profit growth in 21 years. Since Q2 2020, profits have climbed $2.7 trillion, or 131 percent. As a share of GDP they jumped 1.0 percentage point in Q2 to 14.9 percent, an all-time high.

Earnings breadth lived here. Nvidia was the headline.

5. IREN's print is the AI-cloud conversion, with a miss on the old P&L. $IREN Q4 fiscal 2026 revenue $137.2 million, adjusted EBITDA $19.2 million versus $41.1 million expected. AI cloud Q4 $70.5 million from $33.6 million. Fiscal 2026 AI cloud $128.8 million, roughly 8 times year over year. Full-year revenue $707.0 million versus $740 million. Net loss $702.6 million, including $638.8 million of impairments.

The pipeline is the story they want you to take:

  • Operating ARR (annualized recurring revenue) $1 billion
  • Contracted ARR for 2026 capacity $4 billion
  • $2.8 billion of new GPU financing
  • $14 billion of cash and committed financing
  • Recent 3-year contract pricing up 125 percent
  • Talks around $25 million of revenue per IT megawatt

Thursday was Microsoft accepting Horizon 1. Friday is the quarter. EBITDA missed. The AI-cloud line is the one that grew.
