July PCE printed hot and gold faded off $4,700

Headline PCE was plus 0.2 percent on the month against plus 0.1 percent expected, plus 3.7 percent year over year, gold faded to $4,613.10 midday, and the 5 to 7 year buyback took $1.19 billion against a $4 billion cap.

InflationRatesCommoditiesPolicy

The new print is PCE, not yesterday's 30-year feud. Per the Market list, July headline PCE rose 0.2 percent on the month against 0.1 percent expected, after a 0.1 percent decline prior. Year over year it was 3.7 percent against 3.6 percent expected. Core was in line, 0.2 percent month over month and 3.3 percent year over year, the second-highest reading since October 2024 and nearly twice the Fed's 2 percent target. That is today's inflation tape.

Market context. Gold opened just shy of $4,700, a continuation of Tuesday's 15-week high, then faded. The same list has spot gold down nearly 1 percent at $4,613.10 an ounce at Wednesday midday. Per 0xResearch, the dollar index hit its lowest since May, gold is up 14.9 percent on the month, and Tuesday the S&P was up 0.2 percent while Bitcoin was down 0.1 percent. Q2 GDP held at 1.5 percent annualized. GDP price index 6.4 percent against 6.2 percent expected. Real personal spending 0.0 percent.

1. The 5 to 7 year buyback undershot the cap. Per the Financial Markets list, Treasury accepted $1.19 billion in 5 to 7 year debt against a $4 billion maximum, with $8.4 billion offered. That is a different tenor from yesterday's 10s-to-30s doubling from September 10. Operations, not a new yield print. No 30-year number in today's article bodies.

2. Warsh speaks Friday. September hike odds are not one number. 0xResearch has Kevin Warsh's first Jackson Hole keynote as Fed chair on Friday, and September hike odds still near 40 percent. That is a Wednesday newsletter print. Charles Edwards, recorded Monday August 24 and landed in today's Milk Road show, said a 70 percent chance of at least one hike before year end. Monday ClearValue had no-hike odds at 60 percent. I am not flattening those into one figure.

3. Hayes's path is print early, then $250,000. Per Arthur Hayes on Pomp, look up and see Bitcoin at 250,000 after they print early and print often. He calls the buyback upsize like $20 billion over the scheduled amount. Irrelevant, in his words. That is a path after they print. It is not a spot call, and it is not a reopening of yesterday's Bessent-Druck op-ed.

4. What to watch. Friday Warsh at Jackson Hole. September hike still near 40 percent on 0xResearch, against a Monday recording at 70 percent for at least one hike this year. Gold $4,613.10 midday after a $4,700 open. The 5 to 7 year operation that took $1.19 billion against $4 billion.
