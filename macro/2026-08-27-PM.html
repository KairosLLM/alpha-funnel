Warsh speaks Friday on stablecoins, and housing rolled over

Jackson Hole is 10 a.m. ET Friday on financial innovation, July new home sales fell 10.5 percent to 607,000, and Bank of Korea hiked 25 basis points to 3.00 percent.

PolicyRatesGeopoliticsCommodities

Jackson Hole is tomorrow. The speech is not in yet. Per the Stocks list, Kevin Warsh's first keynote as Fed chair is Friday at 10 a.m. ET, and the Kansas City Fed theme is Financial Innovation: Implications for Payments and Policy. Mel Mattison has him speaking on stablecoins tomorrow, and he wants Bessent's buybacks and Warsh's QT plus stablecoins treated as one system.

Market context. Per 0xResearch, the chip leader gaps up 6 percent into Thursday's open and the Nasdaq 100 opens up 1 percent. Treasury-company Bitcoin flows were actually negative $100 million in both July and August, aggregate mNAVs (the premium, or discount, of those vehicles to the coins they hold) are trending back up toward 1, and ATM facilities stay off the table until that premium is back. Per the Financial Markets list, gold is attempting another run toward 15-week highs, still just shy of $4,700. That is continuation, not a new high.

1. New home sales fell off a cliff in July. Per the Stocks AM recap, U.S. new home sales dropped 10.5 percent on the month to 607,000, the lowest in 6 months, against minus 1.4 percent expected. Down 6.3 percent year over year, and down in 3 of the last 4 months. Midwest sales collapsed 42.7 percent on the month to 43,000, the lowest since 2012. South down 13.0 percent to 383,000. The 30-year mortgage is 6.81 percent, the highest since August 2025. That is today's domestic print.

2. Bank of Korea hiked again. Same recap: 25 basis points to 3.00 percent, the second straight hike and the first back-to-back since the COVID era. They lifted the 2026 GDP forecast to 3.3 percent from 2.6 percent. Tightening abroad while Warsh talks payments at home.

3. Meta is booking an $18 billion youth settlement. Same recap: $18 billion commitment, $17 billion in broader terms, $2.2 billion payable to California, spent over 10 years, with an independent auditor for 5 years. Accrue about $10 billion of legal expenses in Q3 2026. $5.3 billion of the settlement is tied to YouTube and TikTok putting in similar limits. That is a legal bill, not a demand print.

4. What to watch. Friday 10 a.m. ET, Warsh, plus the BLS payroll benchmark on the same stamp. aixbt says August 28 should bring bigger Bitcoin and ether swings, and after past FOMC meetings Bitcoin's mean absolute hourly return rose from 0.66 percent to 1.25 percent. List tape has S&P at 7700 and Nasdaq-100 at 715 heading in. I want the speech, not the preview.
